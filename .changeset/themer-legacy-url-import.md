The themer tool imports themes from themer.sanity.build, for a quick way off the hosted Themer service: with the sidebar open, paste a `https://themer.sanity.build/api/hues?…` URL — or any snippet with one in it, like the `import {theme} from '…'` line of a Studio config — anywhere but into a text field, and it becomes one of your own themes, applied to the Studio and open in the editor, ready to copy as a `buildTheme` snippet. The URL is parsed exactly like the hosted service did, presets, overrides and validation errors included, and colors written with a `#` (`lightest=#fff`) work too. The primary hue becomes the accent, the default hue the text color and its lightest and darkest colors the light and dark backgrounds — the way the presets were carried over, so a preset URL converts to the preset of the same name — while the colors the hosted service defaulted to, and the hues `buildTheme` has no counterpart for, are left to the stock Studio colors. An "Import from URL" button below the themes opens a dialog to paste into, with a preview of the converted theme, for touch screens and anyone who would not think of pasting into the sidebar.
